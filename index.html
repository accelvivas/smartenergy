Smart Tips Based on Your Usage Patterns
  • Eliminate Standby Power: Turn off standby appliances (TV, chargers, computer monitors) to save up to 10% on your monthly bill. Estimated savings: ₱135/month.
  • Peak Hour Management: Avoid using high-wattage devices (washing machine, dryer, electric oven) during peak hours (6-9 PM) to reduce costs by 20-30%.
  • LED Upgrade Priority: Switch remaining incandescent/CFL bulbs to LED for 75% energy reduction in lighting. ROI: 6-12 months.
  • AC Optimization: Current AC usage detected at 22°C. Adjust thermostat to 24-25°C for optimal comfort and 10-15% savings without significant comfort loss.
  • Natural Light Utilization: Maximize natural daylight usage between 8 AM - 4 PM. Keep curtains open and delay artificial lighting until necessary.
  • Load Scheduling: Schedule washing machine, dishwasher, and other heavy appliances during off-peak hours (10 PM - 6 AM) for 25% lower rates.
  • Smart Device Integration: Consider adding smart plugs or timers to automate device shutdown during unused hours, especially for entertainment systems.
